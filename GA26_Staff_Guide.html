Getting Started

📱
Open the app on your Android phone Go to sern-ga2026.github.io/programmes/checkin.html in Chrome. For quick access: tap ⋮ → Add to Home Screen to install as an app icon.
1
Enter PIN 2026
The 4-digit code unlocks the staff check-in view. Tap the numbers on screen.
2
Select the current activity
Use the dropdown at the top to choose the session or meal you are managing. The counter shows how many participants are expected vs. arrived.
3
Scan the badge QR or search by name
Tap 📷 Avvia fotocamera QR and point at the badge. Or type the participant's name in the search box.
4
Confirm check-in
The participant card appears. Tap ✓ Check-in to confirm. The counter updates instantly.
2

Scanning Badges

▦

Each badge has a unique QR code

Point the camera at the QR — it reads automatically without pressing anything. The participant card appears within 1–2 seconds.

The same QR links to the participant's personal programme page.

⚠️
Camera not working? Tap the 🔒 lock icon in Chrome's address bar → Camera → Allow → Reload the page. The app requires HTTPS (GitHub Pages URL) for the camera to work.
💡
No camera available? Use the search box — type the first letters of the name or the P-ID (e.g. P042). Tap the name in results to open the card, then tap Check-in.
3

Reading the Participant Card

🗳
Blue "Votante" badge
This person is a designated voter for the formal Assembly. The number shows how many votes they carry. Note this during GA check-in.
🍽️
Orange "Dietary" badge
This participant has a dietary restriction. Tap the badge to see the details. At meals a full banner appears automatically.
⚠️
Yellow warning
The participant is not assigned to the current activity. You can still check them in — use your judgement.
✓
Green "Già registrato" button
Person was already checked in. You can re-confirm — the timestamp updates.
4

Tabs — Presenti & Attesi

✅
Presenti
Everyone checked in so far for the selected activity, with their check-in time.
⏳
Attesi / Mancanti
Expected participants who have not arrived yet. Tap a name to jump straight to their check-in card.
5

Dietary Restrictions at Meals

At lunches and dinners, a full orange banner appears automatically when you check in a participant with a restriction. No need to tap anything — it's always visible.

ExampleRestriction
Lotta Åberg (P006)Gluten free, no scampi
Sofe Sefaj (P051)Pescetarian, no alcohol
Peter Eriksson (P091)No nuts, no strawberries

The full list of restrictions is visible in the app for every participant who has one. The orange banner appears automatically — you do not need to memorise anything.

Formal General Assembly (16:00 Fri)

⚖️
Select activity: "⚖ Formal General Assembly" Check in each participant as they enter the room. Voting members are highlighted with a blue badge.
1
Staff scans all participants at the door
Use PIN 2026 → select "Assemblea Generale Formale" → scan badges.
2
Project the quorum screen
On a tablet or laptop connected to the projector, open the app and enter PIN 0000. The quorum display auto-refreshes every 30 seconds and shows ✅ QUORUM REACHED when the threshold is met.
3
Attendance register
The SERN Secretariat will manage the official register and export it at the end of the session. Your role is to check in participants at the door using PIN 2026.

Troubleshooting

📷
Camera permission denied Tap 🔒 in Chrome address bar → Camera → Allow → Reload. Must be on the https:// URL.
🔍
Participant not found by QR Use the name search instead. If still not found, check the P-ID on the badge and search that.
📵
No internet connection The app works offline using cached data. Check-ins are saved locally and sync when back online.
🗑
Need to reset test data before the event Contact the SERN Secretariat to reset the data before the event. Requires double confirmation. Do this only during setup — never during the live event.
